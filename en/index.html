02 / ROOTS 1987
On the same shore, under the same name, since 1987.

The three wooden tables Captain İsmet set down beside Çökertme Caddesi in the summer of 1987 still face the sea with the same plainness. Our meze list has not grown longer, and we have never compromised on the freshness of the catch.
