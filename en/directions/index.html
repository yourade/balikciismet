I BY ROAD

By Car & Parking

From the centre of Bitez, follow the road down to the shore through the tangerine groves. There is free guest parking in the old olive grove 60 metres behind the restaurant. There is no valet and no loud welcome.

II PUBLIC TRANSPORT & THE SQUARE

Taxi & the Bitez Dolmuş

The Bitez dolmuş (lines 2-48 and 2-49) from Bodrum bus station drops you at the square 100 metres above the restaurant. For the journey back at night, the Bitez taxi rank runs until 02:00.
