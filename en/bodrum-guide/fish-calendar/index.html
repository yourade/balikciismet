Levrek (sea bass)

Dicentrarchus labrax · Bitez and Mandalya Bay

Taken on a longline over deep water rock. Cooked whole over charcoal; nothing touches the flesh but olive oil and bay leaf.

TEXTURE & FAT Firm white flesh, medium fat
MINIMUM LANDING SIZE 25 cm
PEAK: AUGUST – NOVEMBER · DIVING UNDER ROCK

Çipura (gilt-head bream)

Sparus aurata · the Bodrum rock ground

Wild gilt-head bream that feeds on mussels among the Bodrum rocks. Baked in a sea salt crust with the scales on; it comes to the table in its own juices with lemon.

MINIMUM LANDING SIZE 20 cm
IN THE KITCHEN Baked in salt or grilled
ROCK GROUND
BOTTOM LONGLINE & JIG
SQUID: SEPTEMBER – FEBRUARY · OCTOPUS: 1 NOVEMBER – 14 APRIL

Bitez Squid & Rock Octopus

Loligo vulgaris / Octopus vulgaris · Gümbet & the Bitez shore

Octopus legs dried in the sun over rock bottoms, then sealed briefly over oak charcoal. The squid goes into the pan with nothing but a light dusting of flour and tarator, so it keeps the smell of the sea.

OCTOPUS CLOSED SEASON 15 APRIL – 31 OCTOBER
OCTOPUS MINIMUM WEIGHT 750 G
